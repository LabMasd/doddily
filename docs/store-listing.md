### Description
Doddily shows you what's on for your baby or toddler near you today.

Pick a day and see sessions in time order, with how far away each one is. Baby sensory, music, swimming, library rhyme times, free stay and play, toddler football, soft play, farms and parks, all in one place.

- **Today at a glance:** sessions in time order, with walking time
- **Search:** find a class, group or venue by name
- **Right for your child:** add your children's ages and Today only shows what suits them
- **Filters that matter:** free, no booking, rainy day
- **Every detail in one place:** times, price, ages, how to book, address
- **One tap:** directions, the provider's booking page, add to calendar, reminders
- **Map:** everything near you on one map
- **Saved:** keep the ones you like
- **No account:** open it and go

Listings come from providers' own pages, councils and libraries across the UK. Timetables change, so each class links to the provider to check before you go.

### App Privacy ("nutrition label")
- **Data collected:** none ("Data Not Collected").
- **Location:** used on the device to find nearby listings and not stored by us. The postcode lookup goes to postcodes.io and place data is fetched as map tiles; neither is linked to you.
- **Tracking:** none. **Ads:** none. **Accounts:** none.

### Notes for App Review
Doddily lists baby and toddler activities in the United Kingdom only, and needs no login.

If you are outside the UK, "Use where I am" will find nothing nearby. Instead, type the postcode **E8 3PB** (London) on the first screen, or later under You > Where. Today then lists that day's sessions. Tap one for details, directions, add to calendar and reminders. The Map tab shows the same listings on a map.
