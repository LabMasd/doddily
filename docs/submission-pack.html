The order to do it in

Submitting

Wait for Apple now

Usually one to two days. You’ll get an email when review starts and again when it finishes. Nothing to do meanwhile.

If they reject it

Apple writes the reason in Resolution Center. Send it to me and I’ll fix the app or the listing and resubmit. A rejection costs a day, not the launch.

When they approve it

Release is set to manual, so the app waits for you. Pick a morning, press Release, then post the launch post from the playbook and add the App Store link to every bio.

Then open the website up

Remove the noindex line from index.html and privacy.html, and the Disallow from robots.txt, so doddily.app can be found in search. Ask me and it’s a one-minute change.
